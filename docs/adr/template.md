# NNNN. Title in the imperative: what we decided, not the problem

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play? What hurts today, what constraints do we have (technical, team, budget, compliance)?
Write it so someone who joins in a year understands why this was a question at all. Facts, not opinions.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly enough that a reviewer can tell whether a pull request follows it. Include the
defaults and numbers if there are any.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we accept as the cost. Include the follow-up work this creates.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious option with one or two lines on why it lost. "Do nothing" is often one of them.
